01 / Approach

Good analysis starts before the chart.

The tools matter, but the question, data quality, and honest interpretation matter just as much.

I want my work to be clear, checkable, and useful to the person reading it.

My projects are a mix of guided learning and hands-on practice. I try to understand the business question first, inspect the dataset, and validate important calculations before sharing a result. I also try to be clear about assumptions and limitations instead of making claims the data cannot support.

STEP 01

Inspect the data

Check missing values, duplicates, inconsistent labels, data types, and unexpected records.

STEP 02

Answer a question

Use SQL, Excel, Python, or BI measures to investigate a defined question and compare results.

STEP 03

Explain the result

Communicate what the data suggests, what it does not prove, and what deserves a closer look.
